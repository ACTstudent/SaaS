Privacy

Last updated: [date]

Your resumes stay on your device

Everything you type into Inkwell is saved in your browser’s local storage on the device you are using. We don’t have accounts or a server copy of your resumes, and we can’t see, read or recover them. Clearing your browser data deletes them, so use Back up resumes on the Resumes page to keep a copy.

The writing assistant runs on your device

The Resume Writing Assistant is a set of writing rules that runs inside the page. Your text is never sent to us, to an AI service or to anyone else to be checked.

What else is stored

Inkwell is free and has no payments, so we never ask for or receive payment details. The site loads fonts from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address as part of serving them. We don’t use analytics, advertising trackers or cookies.
