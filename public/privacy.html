Privacy

Last updated: [date]

Your resumes stay on your device

Everything you type into Inkwell is saved in your browser’s local storage on the device you are using. We don’t have accounts or a server copy of your resumes, and we can’t see, read or recover them. Clearing your browser data deletes them, so use Back up resumes on the Resumes page to keep a copy.

The writing assistant runs on your device

The Resume Writing Assistant is a set of writing rules that runs inside the page. Your text is never sent to us, to an AI service or to anyone else to be checked.

Payments

The one-time upgrade is paid through PayPal, with a PayPal account, a card or another option PayPal offers. PayPal collects your payment details under its own privacy statement; we never see your card number or PayPal login. After you approve the payment, our server asks PayPal to complete it, checks that it was paid and returns a license key tied to that PayPal order number. PayPal shares your name, email address and order details with us as the seller. We use them only to provide the upgrade, answer support requests and meet legal and tax obligations.

What else is stored

Your license key is kept in your browser alongside your resumes. The site loads fonts from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address as part of serving them. We don’t use analytics, advertising trackers or cookies. The PayPal buttons load from paypal.com only when you open the upgrade window, and PayPal may set its own cookies at that point.
