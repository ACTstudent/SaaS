Frequently asked questions

Where are my resumes stored?

In your browser on this device. There is no account and no server copy. Use Back up resumes on the Resumes page to keep a copy or move to another device.

Does the Writing Assistant use AI?

No. It is a set of writing rules built for resumes that runs on your device, so it works offline and never sends your text anywhere.

Is the upgrade a subscription?

No. You pay $10 once and can keep up to 20 resumes. Nothing renews.

Will applicant tracking systems read my PDF?

Yes. Export keeps your text as real text rather than an image. For the safest result, choose the ATS simple template.

Can I switch templates after I start?

Yes. Your content stays the same and the new layout applies instantly.
